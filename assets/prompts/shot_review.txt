## Quality Criteria

1. **SPECIFICITY**: No generic terms allowed.
   - BAD: "dark figure standing", "mysterious creature"
   - GOOD: "2.4 meter tall emaciated humanoid, alabaster white skin, no pigmentation, extremely long arms reaching past knees, sparse white hair, sharp angular jawline"

2. **CHARACTER COVERAGE**: If the synopsis mentions a character, their physical traits MUST appear in at least one shot prompt. For named/described entities (SCP-096, soldiers, scientists), use concrete physical descriptors.

3. **KEY NARRATIVE ELEMENT**: The most important story element from the synopsis must be visually represented somewhere in the shots. Check: is the key action/object/event actually visible?

4. **VISUAL CONSISTENCY**: All shots in the same scene must share:
   - Same color temperature keywords
   - Same location/environment
   - Same time of day / atmosphere

5. **CROSS-SCENE CONTINUITY** (if previous scene info is provided):
   - Shot #1 of this scene must visually connect to the LAST shot of the previous scene
   - Match: color temperature, lighting style, atmosphere keywords
   - The transition should feel like a natural cut, not a jarring scene change
   - Do NOT copy the previous shot's subject — only carry over the visual atmosphere

6. **REQUIRED SUFFIX**: Every prompt must end with:
   "cinematic still, dark mystery atmosphere, muted color palette, film grain, 16:9 aspect ratio, photorealistic"
